Before you begin, find some paper, or a sketchbook, and a pen or pencil you like. That will be your space.

This app only holds the prompts. What you draw stays on paper, and it’s yours alone.

A reminder, if you like

Inkling can’t send notifications by itself. If you’d like a nudge, you can add a daily reminder to your phone’s calendar.

  1. Tap the button. Your phone downloads a small calendar file.
  2. Open it, and choose to add the events to your calendar.

About & privacy

What this is

Inkling is a small personal project: one drawing prompt at a time, for you to answer on paper. It is free, and has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What is stored, and where

Only what the app needs to work, in your browser’s local storage on your own device:

  • your settings: how many drawings a day, their times, and the timer length;
  • whether you’ve seen the welcome, and whether you’ve added the reminders;
  • your “Not today, another one” choice for the current prompt.

To work offline, your browser also keeps a copy of the app’s own files. None of this is sent anywhere. To delete it, uninstall the app or clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

What you draw

It stays on paper. The app never asks for it, and never uses your camera or microphone.

Hosting and reminders

Wherever this page is hosted may log technical data, such as IP addresses, for security. The calendar file is made on your device and goes only to the calendar app you open it with.
